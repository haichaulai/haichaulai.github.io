Pixwand

AI photo & video editor for iPhone and iPad

Pixwand edits photos and videos right on your device — filters and adjustments, trimming, joining clips, collages, slideshows, AI motion tracking, background blur, object removal and more. Nothing is uploaded: your media never leaves your iPhone or iPad.

Download on the App Store

How to use Pixwand

Five tabs, one for each kind of job. Tap one for a guide with short clips of the app.

  • ✨ Edit Image Filters, adjustments, crop, text
  • 🎞️ Video Photos into a video
  • ✂️ Trim Cut and style one clip
  • 🔗 Join Clips end to end
  • 🔳 Collage Several in one picture

New to the app? Start with the overview — choosing media, moving between tabs, and saving your work.
