Frequently asked questions

Are my photos and videos uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens on your device. Your media is never uploaded to us, and exported files are saved only where you choose. See our Privacy Policy.

What is Pixwand Pro?

Pixwand Pro unlocks the Pro features and removes the watermark. Edit Image and Collage are free. Pro is available as an in-app purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

On your device, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Pixwand, and manage or cancel it there.

An export failed or the app behaved unexpectedly — what should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app, and make sure you have free storage space. If it keeps happening, email us the details above and we'll look into it.

Does the app show ads?

The free version shows ads (Google AdMob). Pixwand Pro removes them.
