Pixwand — Support

Common questions

Where are my edited photos saved?

Tap Save to Photos to write the result to your photo library, or use the Share button to send it to another app.

Why does the app ask for microphone access?

Only if you choose to record a voice-over for a slideshow. Audio is recorded on your device and used as the soundtrack — nothing is uploaded.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All editing runs on your device and works without an internet connection. Ads need a connection, so offline you simply won't see them.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Pixwand free. They are provided by Google AdMob and are shown at natural breaks — for example after you save or export — never on top of the photo or video you are editing.

Why does the app ask for permission to track?

That is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Allowing it lets Google show ads that are more relevant to you; declining still shows ads, just not personalised ones. Your photos and videos are never used for ads. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Privacy choices in the app to review or change your consent. Everywhere, you can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Is there a watermark on exports?

No. Saved and shared files are clean.

How many photos can a slideshow have?

Up to 30. You can set the timing per photo and a transition either for the whole slideshow or after each photo.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. Your photos, videos and recordings never leave your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage information to serve them — the policy explains what and how to opt out.
