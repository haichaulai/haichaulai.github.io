Trim Video

Cut a shorter clip out of a video, then everything else that works on a single clip.

1Choose a video

Tap Choose Video in the header and pick where it comes from — your Photo Library, the Files app, or the Camera. The clip loads with its filmstrip underneath.

2Cut it down

In the Cut tool, drag the two handles on the filmstrip to the part you want. The times above tell you where each handle sits and how long the result will be.

  • Keep — everything between the handles is what you keep.
  • Cut Out — the opposite: the part between the handles is removed.

Tap Trim Selection when the selection is right.

3Looks and effects

The Filters tool carries the same grades as Edit Image, plus one-tap Templates that set a filter, speed and atmosphere together.

The Effects tool is where a clip gets its motion:

  • Reverse, Boomerang, Hold End — play it backwards, bounce it, freeze the last frame.
  • Velocity and Freeze Pop — speed ramps, and a frozen cut-out of the subject.
  • Speed — from slow motion to fast forward, with the new length shown.
  • Motion tracking — a blur or mosaic cover that follows a face or object.

4Everything else on the clip

  • Frame — aspect ratio, background fill, auto-reframe for a vertical cut.
  • Text — captions, stickers and auto-captions from the clip's own speech.
  • Layer — a photo, a second video or a chroma-keyed layer on top.
  • Sound — music, voice-over, voice changer, or mute the original.
  • Export — resolution, frame rate and quality before you save.

Save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
