Photos to Video

Turn a set of photos into a video — a template, music and movement do the work.

1Choose your photos

Tap Choose Photos and pick two or more. They play in the order you picked them, and you can drag them into another order in the Photos tool.

2Pick a template

Open the Templates tool and tap one — Simple, Travel, Birthday, Memories, Romantic, Energetic, Winter, Snapshot and more. A template sets the pace, the movement and the transitions in one tap.

Prefer to set it yourself? Leave the template off and use Filters, Effects and Frame directly.

3Music and movement

  • Sound — add a track from your library, or one of the built-in pieces.
  • Beat sync — once there is music, each photo changes on the beat.
  • Ken Burns — a slow pan or zoom per photo; tap a photo's move to change it.
  • Duration — how long each photo stays on screen.

Then tap Create Video and the finished video appears in the preview.

Save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
