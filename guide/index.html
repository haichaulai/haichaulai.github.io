How to use Pixwand

Three short clips, recorded in the app. No sound — nothing to turn down.

Pixwand edits photos and videos on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and your media never leaves your iPhone or iPad.

1Choose a photo or video

Every tab starts by asking for media. Tap the pill in the header — Choose Video, Choose Photos or Add Clips — and pick where it comes from:

  • Photo Library — anything in Photos. Pixwand only sees what you select.
  • Files — a clip from iCloud Drive, a folder or another app.
  • Camera — take a photo or record a video there and then.

You can also send media to Pixwand from another app's share sheet, or with "Open in Pixwand" from Files.

2Find the right tab

Five tabs sit along the bottom, one for each kind of job:

  • Edit Image — filters, adjustments, retouching, crop, text and markup on a photo.
  • Video — turn a set of photos into a video with music and transitions.
  • Trim — cut a shorter clip out of a video, and everything that works on one clip: effects, speed, captions, overlays.
  • Join — put several clips one after another, with transitions between them.
  • Collage — lay photos or clips out side by side in a grid.

Your work is kept per tab, so moving to another one and back doesn't lose it.

3Apply a look

Open the Filters tool under the preview and tap a thumbnail — the picture changes straight away. Filters come in groups:

  • Looks — finished grades like Cinematic or Golden Hour.
  • Classic — Noir, Vintage, Vibrant, Warm.
  • Creative — stronger, more stylised treatments.
  • My LUTs — .cube files you bring in yourself.

The Intensity slider dials a filter back. Reset clears it, and undo is in the top corner of the preview.

Then save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
