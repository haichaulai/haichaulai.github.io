Join Videos

Put several clips one after another, with a transition between them.

1Add your clips

Tap Add Clips and pick them from your Photo Library or the Files app. The header counts what you have added.

Press and hold a clip in the strip to drag it into another position. Each clip can be trimmed on its own, so the join uses only the part you want.

2Choose the transition

Open the Effects tool and tap the transition menu. Over a hundred are listed — Cut, Fade, Fade to Black, Slide, Zoom, Wipe, Flash, Blur Fade and on — each with an ⓘ saying what it does. The preview plays the change straight away.

The transition you pick applies between the clips; Cut means no transition at all.

3Sound and the finished video

  • Clips — reorder, trim or remove a clip, and set how loud each one is.
  • Sound — one piece of music across the whole thing, or a voice-over.
  • Layer — a photo, sticker or second video over the join.

Tap Join Clips and the finished video appears in the preview.

Save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
