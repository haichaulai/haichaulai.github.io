Edit Image

Everything that happens to a single photo: looks, adjustments, retouching, crop and markup.

1Open a photo

Tap Choose Photo in the header and pick where it comes from — your Photo Library, the Files app, or the Camera. Pixwand only sees the photos you select.

A photo sent from another app's share sheet, or "Open in Pixwand" from Files, lands here too.

2Pick a look

Open the Filters tool under the preview and tap a thumbnail — the photo changes as you tap. They come in groups:

  • Looks — finished grades like Cinematic or Golden Hour.
  • Classic — Noir, Vintage, Vibrant, Warm.
  • Creative — stronger, more stylised treatments.
  • My LUTs — .cube files you bring in yourself.

The Intensity slider dials a filter back; Reset clears it. Undo and redo sit in the top corner of the photo.

3The other tools

The row under the photo scrolls — each tool opens its own panel:

  • Adjust — exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness, vignette.
  • Beauty — skin smoothing and retouching on a face.
  • Background — blur, replace or erase what's behind the subject.
  • Frame — borders and framing around the picture.
  • Crop — straighten, rotate, flip, or crop to a ratio.
  • Text and Draw — captions, stickers and markup on top.
  • Remove — paint over something and it goes.
  • Enhance, Sky, Funny — upscaling, a new sky, fun-house faces.

Save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
