Collage

Several photos — or clips — side by side in one picture.

1Add photos or clips

Tap Add Media and pick two or more. Videos are welcome: a collage with a clip in it becomes a video you can export.

Drag a cell to swap two pictures around; pinch inside a cell to move and zoom the picture within it.

2Lay it out

The Layout tool holds the grids — the number of cells follows how many pictures you added. Tap a layout to switch to it; what is in each cell stays.

3Style it

The Style tool is where a collage stops looking like a grid:

  • Shape — the whole picture's ratio: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and others.
  • Collage shape — rounded, circle, heart, star, hexagon, flower and more.
  • Border — width, corner rounding and colour between the cells.
  • Background — a colour, a gradient, or one of your own photos behind it.
  • Text — captions and stickers over the finished collage.

Save or share

Save writes the result to your photo library. Share opens the places it can go — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messages, or anywhere else through the system share sheet. Pixwand Pro removes the watermark and unlocks the Pro tools.
