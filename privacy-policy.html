Privacy Policy — Pixwand

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Pixwand is a photo and video editor. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your photos, videos and recordings never leave your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

Your photos and videos

  • You choose photos and videos with Apple's system picker. The app only receives the items you pick.
  • All editing — filters, adjustments, crop and markup, slideshow rendering, trimming — happens entirely on your device. Your media is never uploaded anywhere and is never shared with advertisers or anyone else.
  • Edited results are written to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos, or handed to another app only when you use the system Share sheet. That requires the "Add to Photos" permission and nothing more.
  • Temporary working files are kept in the app's private storage while you work and are deleted by the app.

Microphone

If you record a voice-over for a slideshow, the app asks for microphone access and records audio only while you are actively recording. The recording is saved on your device and used as the slideshow's soundtrack. It is never uploaded or shared by the app.

Advertising

Pixwand is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We do not run our own analytics and we do not receive personal information about you; AdMob only gives us aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers — the Identifier for Advertising (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below), and other app-level identifiers.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city — not your precise location).
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, app version.
  • Ad interactions — which ads were shown and whether they were tapped.
  • Diagnostics — ad load times and errors.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking permission (iPhone and iPad). The app asks for permission before the IDFA can be used to personalise ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's consent message so you can choose how your data is used for advertising. You can review or change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the app.
  • Personalised ads in general. You can manage ad personalisation from Google at adssettings.google.com.

Network

Editing works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to load ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK; if you are offline, ads simply don't appear and everything else keeps working.

Children

Pixwand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone; ad requests are handled by Google as described above.
