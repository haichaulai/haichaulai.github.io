Privacy Policy

Pixwand · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pixwand app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We designed Pixwand to do its editing work on your device, and we collect as little as possible.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

Pixwand is an on-device photo and video editor. The photos, videos, and audio you open, edit, or export are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or have access to your media, and we never send it to our servers (we do not operate media servers). Exported files are saved only where you choose (your Photos library or the Files app).

Information collected

We use the following third-party services, which collect limited data to help us run and improve the App:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage and diagnostics data, such as which screens and features are used, app events, crash and performance data, and device/app information (device model, OS version, language, and a device/installation identifier). This is used to understand how the App is used and to improve it.
  • Google AdMob — serves advertising in the free version of the App and may use advertising identifiers and related data to measure and (where permitted) personalize ads.
  • Apple (App Store & In-App Purchases) — if you buy Pixwand Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We receive only the subscription/purchase status needed to unlock Pro features; we never receive your payment details.

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, or precise location, and we do not require an account to use the App.

Purposes

  • Analytics — to understand feature usage and fix problems.
  • Advertising — to show ads in the free version.
  • App functionality — to deliver and unlock purchased features.

Tracking and your choices

On first launch (or in iOS Settings), you can allow or decline app tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can also limit ad personalization in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and reset your advertising identifier at any time. Declining tracking does not stop you from using the App.

Third-party policies

  • Google (Firebase & AdMob): policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Data retention and security

Analytics and advertising data are retained by Google according to their policies. Because we do not run our own servers, we do not store personal data ourselves. We use the privacy and security controls provided by Apple and Google.

Children

Pixwand is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
